Development

Fiesta Heroes began when a group of people who cared about the game joined together to provide a space for the community to come together. We develop, maintain, and fix retail and base content across sets including NA2016, CN2012, and TW2008, so you can start developing immediately.

Our team works to keep the process seamless. Report issues through our GitHub or Discord; we do our best to address them and create a stable base.
